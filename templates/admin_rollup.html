Academic Year Roll-Up (Financial)

Carry forward outstanding student balances to the next academic year.

Critical Operation Notice

  • This will identify every student with a non-zero balance in the source year.
  • A new CHARGE entry (Arrears) will be created in the destination year.
  • Both positive balances (Arrears) and negative balances (Overpayments) will be carried forward.
  • This operation cannot be automatically undone. Please confirm your selection.

Balances will be posted as the starting balance in this year.

Why is this necessary?

In school accounting, balances from the previous session (e.g., unpaid tuition or credit balances) must be reflected as the opening position in the new session. This tool automates that transfer into the Arrears ledger for accurate invoicing.
